Design Automation

## Engineering Copilot

Engineering Copilot reads your existing CAD models, understands how they were designed, and lets your engineers change a design and get updated parts and assemblies. It runs on your own CAD platform, from any CAD software.

#### Reads Your CAD Data

The software reads and analyses your parts, assemblies and relevant data.

#### Establishes Design Logic

It captures the design logic and relations between parts, and shows them on a screen for the user to review or select as inputs.

#### Talk to It in Plain Language

Built-in natural language (NLP) lets engineers ask for changes the way they would tell a colleague.

#### Verifies and Redesigns

When the user changes an input, the system checks the logic and redesigns the affected parts and assemblies.

#### Create New or Update Existing

The user decides: create new parts and assemblies, or update the existing ones.

Example – Pick and Place

The user changes an input. The copilot verifies the logic and updates the parts and assemblies.

FAQs

## Questions engineers ask first

Which CAD software does it work with?

It runs on your own CAD platform, from any CAD software. There is no change of software and no new CAD system to learn.

What does it read from our CAD data?

It reads and analyses your parts, assemblies and relevant data. It then captures the design logic and the relations between parts, and shows them on screen so your engineers can review them or select them as inputs.

How do engineers ask for a change?

In plain language. Built-in natural language (NLP) lets engineers ask for changes the way they would tell a colleague. When an input changes, the system checks the logic and redesigns the affected parts and assemblies.

Does it change our existing models?

Your engineer decides. They can create new parts and assemblies, or update the existing ones.

What if the design needs analysis?

If the design needs FEA or CFD type analysis, the software performs the analysis and produces the results for you.

Who gets the most from it?

Engineering companies with years of CAD libraries and repeat or customized designs, such as machine design and motion automation work.
